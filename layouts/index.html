How we work

Three steps. Fixed-price discovery. No open-ended retainers unless you ask for one.

01

Discovery Call

A 1-hour conversation. Your stack, your constraints, the goal. We leave with a sharp problem statement — or we tell you this isn't a fit and refer someone better.

02

Fixed-Price Audit

A scoped 1–3 week deep-dive: architecture review, risk register, cost-optimization roadmap, compliance gap analysis. You get a written deliverable you can act on — with or without us.

03

Engagement

If we both want to keep going: fixed-scope implementation, embedded staff-augmentation, or fractional architect retainer. We own the outcome — not just the design doc.
