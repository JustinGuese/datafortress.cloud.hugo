### NavicareNow: Intelligent Patient Navigation for German Healthcare

NavicareNow is an AI-driven platform that removes friction from the patient journey — guiding users from symptom assessment through to the right specialist, clinic, or emergency service. Built specifically for Germany's multi-payer, multi-provider healthcare landscape.

As CTO and co-architect, Justin Güse designed the underlying AI inference pipeline, cloud infrastructure (Kubernetes), and GDPR-compliant data architecture.

### Key Features

#### AI-Powered Symptom Triage
- **Conversational symptom checker** — natural-language intake that maps symptoms to likely conditions and urgency levels.
- **Smart routing** — directs patients to the most appropriate care pathway (GP, specialist, emergency, telehealth).
- **Multilingual** — supports German and English for Germany's diverse population.

#### Compliance-Grade Infrastructure
- **GDPR & German healthcare data law compliant** — no patient data used for model training without explicit consent.
- **Kubernetes-hosted** — high availability with zero-downtime deployments.
- **Audit trail** — full logging for regulatory review.

#### Provider Integration
- **Appointment booking** — direct scheduling with partner clinics and specialists.
- **Insurance-aware** — routes based on patient's statutory or private insurance coverage.

### The Tech Stack

Built on: Python FastAPI · Kubernetes · PostgreSQL · Redis · Flutter (mobile) · React (web) · German-hosted LLMs for sensitive inference.
