### PsychDiary: Your Private AI Wellbeing Companion

PsychDiary is an AI-powered mental health journal that helps users understand their emotional patterns through intelligent mood tracking, reflection prompts, and personalised insights.

### Key Features

#### Private-First AI
- **On-device processing** — your journal entries never leave your device without consent.
- **GDPR-compliant** — built with European data protection standards from day one.

#### Intelligent Emotional Tracking
- **Mood journaling** — structured daily entries with AI-guided reflection prompts.
- **Pattern recognition** — surfaces emotional trends over time so you can act before burnout sets in.
- **Personalised insights** — AI analyses your entries to identify triggers, progress, and growth areas.

#### Built for Real Life
- **Cross-platform** — available on iOS and Android via Flutter.
- **Offline-first** — fully functional without an internet connection.
- **Therapist-friendly** — exportable reports that can supplement professional mental health care.

### Why PsychDiary?

Most wellbeing apps focus on streaks and superficial metrics. PsychDiary focuses on *understanding* — giving users the same quality of reflective insight you'd expect from a skilled therapist, at any hour of the day.
