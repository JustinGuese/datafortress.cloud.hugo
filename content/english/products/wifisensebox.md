### WiFiSenseBox: When Off-the-Shelf Sensors Don't Cut It

WiFiSenseBox is an end-to-end IoT product combining custom hardware, embedded firmware, and a cloud telemetry backend. It's purpose-built for deployments where commercial sensor packages either don't exist or don't fit the constraints.

#### Capabilities

- **WiFi probe detection** for anonymous foot-traffic and occupancy analytics.
- **Environmental sensors** - temperature, humidity, particulate matter, motion, light.
- **Edge processing** to filter and aggregate before backhaul, reducing bandwidth and cloud cost.
- **OpenObserve-backed analytics** for sub-second metric queries and alerting.

#### Use Cases

Smart-building occupancy, retail foot-traffic analytics, industrial environmental monitoring, and bespoke research deployments where the experimenter needs full control of the hardware and data pipeline.
