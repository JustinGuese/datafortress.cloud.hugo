## Global Fleet Synchronization: A Case Study

**The Challenge (HPE / BMW)**: Establishing global alignment and real-time data compliance across massive data lakes for one of the world's leading automotive giants. The system needed to manage hundreds of petabytes of data across a worldwide network of virtual machines and file-sharing infrastructure.

**The Solution**: We programmed and deployed a hybrid strategy that seamlessly integrated cloud storage providers (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure) with on-premises HPE deduplication servers.

**The Result**: A petabyte-scale data replication pipeline active across more than **60 countries** simultaneously. We overcame bandwidth limitations, significantly reduced storage costs, and ensured 99.95% availability for global system compliance.
