## Banking & BaFin-Regulated Infrastructure

### [Atruvia - DevOps Modernization & Banking Platform Engineering](/portfolio/atruvia-devops-modernization/)

Multi-year DevOps engagement at the IT backbone for Germany's 120+ cooperative banks. **Jenkins-to-GitLab CI migration** across hundreds of pipelines, **Java Spring Boot microservices** on OpenShift, and the **secure Finanzamt integration layer** between core banking and the federal tax authority. All on a defense-in-depth architecture with Istio service-mesh geographic locking and Vault-driven secrets.

### [Atruvia / Volksbank - Data Warehouse Modernization](/portfolio/atruvia--volksbank-data-warehouse/)

Reduced data-warehouse operating cost by 45% across Germany's largest cooperative banking network (120+ banks). Migration from legacy DB2/Teradata stack to a Trino + object-storage architecture with full audit lineage.
